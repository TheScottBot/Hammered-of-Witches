**The Hammer of Witches, hammered.**

The *Malleus Maleficarum* (1486) is one of the most infamous books ever printed: two inquisitors' manual for finding, trying and punishing witches. It also reads like it was written by a drunk guy at 3am. Witches keep stolen members in birds' nests and feed them oats. A witch can milk a knife stuck in a wall. If she can't cry on cue, she's guilty.

This bot quotes it, from Montague Summers' 1928 translation, with a link to each passage's page in the scanned book.

**Commands**

🧹 `/hammered`: a random piece of witch-finding advice, or `/hammered about:milk` for one on a topic

🔥 `/accuse @friend`: the Malleus's evidence that your friend is a witch (named, never pinged)

⚙️ `/setup`: choose what your server sees

📜 `/help`: everything else

**Your server, your taste**

The book is soaked in misogyny, torture, antisemitism and worse. Instead of cutting it for everyone, every passage is tagged, and each server picks what to keep, per category: **off**, **mild** or **full**. Use `/setup lgbt level:off` for an LGBTQ+-friendly server, `/setup gore level:mild` to tone down the torture, or one preset from 🕊️ **Tame** to 🔥 **Unabridged**. Keep the bird's-nest lore, lose the bigotry. New servers start on ⚖️ **Tasteful**.

**Privacy**

No messages read, no user data stored. Only each server's filter choices, deleted when the bot leaves.

*It's history and dark comedy, for laughing at the Malleus, not with it.*
